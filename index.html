Questions

Where do I get my VX Kongz 3D file?

Go to the official CyberKongz website: https://app.cyberkongz.com/kongzhub?type=allAssets

  1. Click "Kongz Hub", then open the VX tab.
  2. Click the VX Kong you want printed.
  3. At the bottom right, click "3D/PFP".
  4. Change the file type to "FBX" and download it.
Do I have to own the NFT?

Yes. We only print pieces held by the owner. So every print traces back to its real holder.

Am I allowed to print someone else's NFT?

Only if you get permission from the owner, and you will need their VX Kongz 3D file.

How long does it take, and where do you ship?

Ships from the USA! Packages are carefully packed and insured. If your item arrives damaged, send us a photo and we will send you another one. We ship worldwide!
